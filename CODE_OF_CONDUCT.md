# Code of Conduct

Belfort follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, especially with beginners; assume good faith; criticise ideas, not people; no harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

Two rules specific to this project:

- **No stock tips or promotion** in issues, discussions or pull requests. Do not name a stock to suggest its price will move, and do not advertise paid groups, courses or services.
- **No collection of other people's financial or personal data.**

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub (the contact on the maintainers' profiles). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and block participants who break this code.
