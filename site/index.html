Tip Check

Got a stock tip on WhatsApp? Check it in 10 seconds.

Paste the message or a screenshot. Belfort lists the red flags it finds, so you can see what a tip is hiding before you act on anything. It reports warning signs. It never tells you to buy or sell.

  • Is the sender SEBI-registered?
  • Are returns promised, or is there pressure to hurry?
  • Is the stock on an NSE surveillance list?
  • Does the price and volume pattern look like a pump?

Practice, research, news

Everything you need, nothing at risk.

Practice

Paper trade with virtual points. Set a stop, size the position, then review every trade. Points have no money value.

Research

One clear report for any NSE stock: a rule-based checklist, the risks and a sensible position size.

News, explained

Market headlines in plain language. Hover over a term to see what it means.
