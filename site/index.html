What is Belfort?

The practice room
for Indian stocks.

Belfort is a free, open-source app that helps you learn, practise and research the Indian stock market. Everything runs on your own computer.

Download for Windows View on GitHub →

Free forever · MIT licence · No account needed

Four parts. One app.

Everything a new trader needs, in one place.

01

Learn

A short, practical course for swing and positional trading. Five-minute lessons with a quiz after each.

02

Practice

Paper trade with virtual points, log predictions, and drill on a chart-replay trainer that hides the future.

03

Research

One clear report for any NSE stock: a rule-based checklist, the risks, and a sensible position size.

04

News

Market headlines in plain language. Hover over any term to see what it means.

Paper trading

Make your mistakes for free.

Place trades with virtual points. Set a stop, size the position, then review every trade to see what you did right and wrong. No real money, ever.

Private by design

Your computer. Your data.

No Belfort server and no sign-up. Your practice records stay on your machine. Bring your own AI key if you want the explainer chat, or use a local model.
