Research a stock before you risk money on it.

Search any NSE company, or start from a screen. Each stock is checked against a fixed, published rule: which conditions hold, the main risk, the price that proves an idea wrong, and how many shares fit your risk limit. You make every decision.

Position size

These two numbers decide how many shares you can hold without losing more than you chose if the stop is hit. They do not change the label.

Learn only what you use.

A practical syllabus for swing and positional trading in Indian shares. Short lessons, a quiz at the end of each, and something to try in Belfort straight away. Nothing you do not need for a real decision.

Practise with points, not money.

A paper account, a prediction journal that scores how honest your confidence is, and a chart-replay trainer. Virtual points only: they have no money value and cannot be redeemed.

The market, explained.
