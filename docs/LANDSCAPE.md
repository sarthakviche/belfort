# The landscape and what Belfort borrows

A short review of products in the same space (November 2026), what each does well, and the gap Belfort aims at.

| Product | What it does well | What Belfort borrows | Where Belfort differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zerodha Varsity** | Free, structured modules with chapters, quizzes at each level, a certificate, streaks and badges in the app | Modules, then lessons, then a quiz; streaks; free forever | Varsity teaches a broad syllabus including F&O and theory. Belfort screens topics to what a cash-market swing or positional trader uses, and every lesson ends inside a practice tool. |
| **StockGro and similar social simulators** | Gamified paper trading that beginners enjoy | Low-friction practice with virtual money | No leagues, prizes or real-money elements (Online Gaming Act, 2025). Scoring rewards process and calibration, not returns. |
| **TradingView** | Best-in-class charts, paper trading, bar replay | Bar replay as a training tool | Belfort's replay hides the company, forces a stop before entry, and scores in R multiples. |
| **Sensibull, AlgoTest** | Options strategy building and virtual options trading | | Out of scope: Belfort does not teach F&O. |
| **Screener.in, Tickertape, Trendlyne** | Fundamentals, screens, peer comparison | Transparent screen criteria; peer P/E | Belfort combines the business check with the chart rule and shows how reliable each signal has been. |
| **Finshots, Mint explainers, Inshorts** | Plain-language news, short and readable | Short summaries; explaining jargon | Belfort explains any headline on demand with the user's own AI key, underlines every term with a hover definition, and hides tip-style headlines. |
| **Duolingo** | Habit loops: short lessons, instant feedback, streaks | Five-to-eight-minute lessons, instant quiz feedback, streaks | |
| **Forecasting communities (Good Judgment, Metaculus)** | Scoring forecasts with proper scoring rules | The Brier score and calibration chart | Applied to a beginner's own stock calls, privately. |
| **Open source: PKScreener, Foursight, OpenAlgo** | Screening, simple paper trading, broker automation | | None of them combines learning, practice and research in one local app. |

## The gap

Beginners in India are taught either **too much theory** (courses) or **too little process** (tips, influencers, gamified simulators). Nobody gives them, in one free and open place:

1. a short, practical syllabus that leaves out what they do not need,
2. a way to practise every lesson immediately, with honest scoring (R multiples, calibration),
3. research tools that show their evidence instead of a verdict, and
4. news explained in plain words, without stock tips.

That is Belfort: **the practice room, not the tip line.**
