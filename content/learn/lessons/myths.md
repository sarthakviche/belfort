Markets are full of stories that sound true. Each myth below has a cost, and each has an earlier lesson that tests it.

## 1. "You can get rich quick"

Social media shows the winners and hides the rest. SEBI's own studies found that about 9 in 10 individual traders in futures and options lost money in each year from 2021-22 to 2023-24, and that about 7 in 10 individual intraday traders in shares lost money in 2022-23. The survivors are the ones who keep the risk per trade small and stay in the game. Slow compounding with controlled losses is the plan that survives.
*Test it:* the lessons on position sizing, R multiples and risk of ruin show how quickly big bets wipe out an account.

## 2. "Indicators predict the next move"

RSI, MACD and moving averages are summaries of past prices. They describe momentum; they do not know what happens next. Belfort's own backtest of chart patterns found no clear edge over random entries.
*Test it:* the lessons on RSI and MACD and on chart pattern evidence.

## 3. "There is a secret method"

Courses sell hidden systems: astrology, Gann angles, Elliott wave counts and "operator" levels. They share a trick: the rules are vague enough that any outcome can be explained afterwards. Belfort leaves them out because there is no reliable evidence that they work. If a method cannot be written as a rule you can test on old charts, you cannot know whether it works.
*Test it:* write the rule down, as in the lesson on writing your own rules, then use the replay trainer.

## 4. "Averaging down always works"

Buying more as a share falls lowers your average price, but it also puts more money into a trade that is already wrong. If the stock keeps falling, you lose more. This is the "moved the stop" mistake in a friendlier name.
*Test it:* the lessons on stop-loss, drawdown rules and biases.

## 5. "It is a good company, so the stock must go up"

A good business can have an expensive share price, and a cheap one can stay cheap. The price depends on what is expected, not only on quality. A strong company can fall for months.
*Test it:* the lessons on the five numbers and on P/E against peers.

## 6. "Big accounts and influencers know"

A large following is not skill, and not a record. Giving advice for a fee needs SEBI registration; anyone can post. Praise, screenshots and follower counts say nothing about results.
*Test it:* the previous two lessons, on tip scams and on checking registration.

## A habit that catches myths

Before acting on any claim, ask three questions:

1. Can this be written as a rule and tested on old data?
2. What would prove it wrong, and what is my stop?
3. Who gains if I believe it?

Sources: SEBI's studies of individual traders' results in equity futures and options (January 2023 and September 2024) and in intraday cash trading (July 2023), published on sebi.gov.in; Belfort's own pattern backtest and lessons referred to above; SEBI's October 2026 warning on invitation-only groups and its registration rules, as described in the previous lessons.
