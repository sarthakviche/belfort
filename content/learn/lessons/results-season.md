Listed companies publish financial results every quarter. Under SEBI's listing rules, quarterly results are due within 45 days of the quarter's end, and the audited results for the last quarter of the financial year within 60 days. Most companies announce the board meeting date on the exchange beforehand, so the date is usually known in advance.

## Why results matter for a short trade

Results often come out after market hours or during the session, and the next open reflects them all at once. A stock can open several percent away from the previous close, past your stop. The loss is then bigger than 1R, as the lesson on gaps showed.

## What Belfort checks

| Style | Results rule |
|---|---|
| Swing (2 to 15 days) | The check fails if results are expected within the next 5 trading days |
| Positional (weeks to months) | Shown for information only, because longer holds usually cross a results date |

If the source has no date, the report says so and asks you to check the exchange's event calendar.

## Three ways to plan around a results date

1. **Exit before:** close the trade before the announcement and look again afterwards.
2. **Size for the gap:** hold through it, but with fewer shares, so a gap twice your stop distance still costs about 1% of capital.
3. **Wait:** do not start a new swing trade until the results are out.

Each choice gives something up: exiting may miss a move, and holding risks a gap. The point is to choose before the date arrives, not on the morning of a gap.

Source: SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, Regulation 33.
